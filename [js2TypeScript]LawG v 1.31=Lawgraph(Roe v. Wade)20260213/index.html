📊 Stage 2: Dobbs v. Jackson (2022)

X/Y positions swap! X (Pink, ↑Up) wins, overruling Roe v. Wade

Majority Opinion (6-3) — Overrules Roe

  • Constitution does not confer right to abortion
  • Roe was "egregiously wrong from the start"
  • Authority returned to the people and their elected representatives

Dissenting Opinion (3 Justices)

  • Overruling Roe undermines the Court's legitimacy
  • Women lose fundamental constitutional protection
  • Stare decisis abandoned without justification
